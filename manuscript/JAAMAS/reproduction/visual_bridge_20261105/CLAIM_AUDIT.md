# Skeptical claim audit before final results
- +0.735 is conditional pipeline performance, original PC is an exact null; fresh rerolls of PF do not turn it into the same receiver. State and visual studies are complementary and one common visual system now supplies both audit types.
- Fresh content replaces the packet only, retaining q/d and candidate functions. Any delayed-decoder mismatch is a scope limitation, not grounds for relabeling it optimal freshness value.
- Absence includes the fixed fallback and cannot be called pure private-information value, train-support robustness or acquisition savings.
- Raw actions and option names are separate dependence endpoints. No sampled lack of changes certifies global nondependence for a nonzero gate.
- F−S confidence intervals across zero mean benefit unresolved, not no effect. Button0 has a structural explanation; bootstrap degeneracy alone proves no equivalence.
- Door success is a native coordinate band. Loss of a flag does not identify door closure; dense reward persists due to different shaping. Ever/terminal/time occupancy should be described, not ranked as universally correct objectives.
- Nonzero-delay state subsets are posthoc, all shown. Door16's barely positive95% interval is not promoted to confirmatory significance.
- Full3seeds share data, initialization, development schedule/capacity. They assess conditional training randomness, not dataset or task distribution generalization.
- Offline H/G has correlated system-environment records, separate denominators, includes all failures, cannot be deployed or bound message routing information.
- New audits are code-based, not independent human verification or clean-machine reproduction. No actual submission/public release or author approval is implied.
