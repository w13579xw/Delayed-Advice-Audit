Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript, ``Auditing the Value of Delayed Advice in Autonomous Agents,'' by Junxiang Wang and Jiaoyang Su, for publication as a Regular Paper in \emph{Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems}.

The paper addresses a problem in evaluating advice-taking agents: a performance gain between separately trained policies need not reflect information in the returned expert message. In visual manipulation, a packet-input variant improved mean reward by 0.735 while its deployed zero predictor rejected every intervention. We explain this counterexample through an executable null-policy certificate.

Our evaluation framework separates fitted-pipeline performance, runtime message dependence and measured outcomes under specified message-channel interventions. Paired native simulation branches connect the original visual system to channel outcomes and complement three state-task studies. An independent generation-time control distinguishes decoder correction from advice value. Candidate-action headroom and three complete visual training systems further examine intervention failures and conditional training variability. These results support reliable evaluation of agent decision-making without claiming a universally better controller.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. There are no related prior publications or preprints. Both authors have reviewed and approved the manuscript and its submission. No funding was received, and the authors declare no competing interests. The manuscript includes a concise disclosure of the computational assistance used. The accompanying Information Sheet answers the journal's four questions.
